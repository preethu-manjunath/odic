## Step 2: Create the shortcut
1. Open the **Shortcuts** app.
2. Tap **+** at the top right.
3. Tap **New Shortcut** at the top → **Rename** → type `ODSC Note`.

## Step 3: Add 5 actions
For each one, tap **Search Actions** at the bottom, type the name in bold, and tap it.

**① Text**
Tap inside the empty box → **Paste**.

**② Split Text**
Check that it reads "Split **Text** by **New Lines**". If it doesn't, tap the blue word after "by" and choose **New Lines**.

**③ Choose from List**
Tap the small **›** arrow on the action, then tap **Prompt** and type `Which session?`

**④ Ask for Input**
Tap the blue word **Prompt** and type `Your note`.

**⑤ Create Note**
Tap inside its text box. A row of blue variable buttons appears above the keyboard:
- tap **Chosen Item**, then press **return**
- tap **Current Date**, then press **return**
- tap **Provided Input**

If you don't see one of them, scroll that blue row sideways.

Tap **Done** at the top right.

## Step 4: Make it a double-tap
**Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Back Tap → Double Tap → ODSC Note**

## Step 5: Test it
1. Double-tap the back of your phone.
2. Tap **9:00 · Dave Bechberger…**
3. Type `test` → **Done**.
4. Open **Notes**. You should see a note starting with "9:00 · Dave Bechberger". Delete it.
